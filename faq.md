# Acme Customer FAQ

## How do I reset my password?

Go to Settings > Security and click "Reset password". We will email you a reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 24 hours, so request a new one if it no longer works.

## How do I track my order?

Open My Account > Orders and select the order. Once it has shipped you will see the courier, the tracking number and the estimated delivery date. We also email you a tracking link on the day it ships.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel an order free of charge until it is marked as "Dispatched". After that, the order can no longer be changed, but you can return it once it arrives.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept cheques, cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

## How do I return an item?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging. Personalised items, gift cards and opened hygiene products (such as earphones) cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Returns are free for customers in the US; international customers pay the return postage.

To start a return:

1. Open My Account > Orders and select the order containing the item.
2. Click "Return item", choose the item and tell us the reason for the return.
3. Print the prepaid return label we email you, or show the QR code at any drop-off point.
4. Pack the item securely, attach the label and drop it off within 14 days.

Once the item reaches our warehouse we will inspect it within 3 working days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and usually appear in your account within 5-7 business days after that.

## How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery (3-5 working days) is free on orders over $50 and $4.99 otherwise. Express delivery (next working day if ordered before 2pm) costs $9.99.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International delivery usually takes 7-14 working days. Import duties and taxes may be charged by your country's customs authority and are paid by the customer.

## How do I contact customer service?

You can reach us by live chat on our website from 8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday, or by emailing support@acme.example. We aim to reply to all emails within one working day.

## Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards are available from $10 to $500. They are delivered by email and are valid for 24 months from the date of purchase.

## How do I unsubscribe from marketing emails?

Click the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any marketing email, or turn off marketing emails in Settings > Notifications. Order and delivery emails will still be sent.

## Is my personal data safe?

We encrypt your data in transit and at rest and never sell it to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details of what we collect and how long we keep it.
